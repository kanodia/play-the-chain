How it works

01C++, unchangedStockfish, Box2D and Bullet are compiled to WebAssembly with icpp-pro. Their engine code isn't rewritten.
0213 computers agreeEach move or shot runs on all 13 nodes of an Internet Computer subnet, and the result is agreed by consensus.
03The game paysCompute is paid in cycles from the canister's own balance (reverse gas), so players need no wallet and pay nothing.

⛽ Fuel the chain

Everyone plays on a shared tank of cycles

Anyone can top up the tank with ICP. The canister turns it into cycles itself, through the IC's cycles minting canister, and the fuel keeps every game free for everybody.
